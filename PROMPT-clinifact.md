Dataset: https://github.com/ds4dh/CliniFact

The data is in that repository under `data/processed/primary_outcome_publication_dataset/`: `train_set.csv`, `validation_set.csv`, and `test_set.csv` (each 22 columns). The task (3-class classification). A clinical research claim is a logical statement of the form "<Intervention> is <Superiority | Non-Inferiority | Equivalence> to <Comparator> in terms of <Outcome>". Given the claim and its linked abstract, predict one label:
- Evidence — the abstract reports results that SUPPORT the claim (a statistically significant primary outcome, p < 0.05).
- Inconclusive — the abstract reports results that do NOT support the claim.
- NEI (Not Enough Information) — the abstract provides only background/context, not results about this claim.
