Below a series of papers on red noise (also knows as stochastic low-frequency variability). Please have a read, look further into the relevant literature, and extract all the relevant data about red noise (in particular look at temperature, luminosity, nu_char (characteristic frequency), the power of the variability, and metallicity. The data is often provided in tables, sometimes you might have to download it from some repository. Aggregate and study possible correlations in the data. 

Compare the trend in the data with existing trends in different theoretical predictions for the origin of red noise. 

Draw conclusions on what is the most likely physical origin of this phenomena.
